Official Policy

Interna / App version 1.0.5

Privacy Policy

Interna is built with an offline-first architecture. Your duty logs, shift hours, photo proofs, learning notes, and generated reports remain securely on your device. No user account is required, and ordinary training data is never synced to a central server.

01

Core Principles

Overview & Architecture

100% Offline-First

Profile info, shift logs, timers, attendance, and proof photos are saved directly to local device storage.

Zero Routine Cloud Sync

Interna operates without a backend user account. Your daily training data stays in your possession.

Optional Online Features

Internet access is used only when you explicitly generate AI reports or interact with optional rewarded ads.

No Tracking or Audio Recording

Interna does not track precise geolocation, access microphone audio, or sell personal data to advertisers.

02

Data Handling

Data Storage & Network Transmission

Profile & OJT Configuration

Student name, course, school, company name, supervisor, required hours, OJT date range, and theme preference stored locally.

Duty Logs & Timer Data

Task descriptions, quantities, dates, time-in and time-out records, and active stopwatch timer sessions stored locally.

Proof Photos & Captions

Photos captured or selected for duty documentation are kept in app sandboxed storage. Proof photos are never transmitted to AI services.

Reports & Learning Reflections

Generated narrative summaries, weekly learning notes, and custom reflection logs saved to local device storage.

AI Report Synthesis (Optional Online)

When you request AI generation, selected text (task logs and learning notes) is sent via Supabase Edge Function to Google Gemini (or xAI/NVIDIA retries). The prompt is processed ephemerally and not retained in a database.

Rewarded Ads & Device Integrity (Optional Online)

Watching rewarded ads interacts with Google Mobile Ads. Pseudonymous install tokens and transaction hashes validate credit rewards via Supabase to prevent fraud.

03

Hardware & System Access

Device Permissions & Security

  • Camera & Photo Library: Requested only when you choose to take or attach proof photos to duty entries.
  • Local Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used exclusively to schedule on-device shift start/end alerts and long-running timer reminders. No remote push tokens are used.
  • Biometrics (Face ID / Fingerprint / Device PIN): Used only for the optional app lock. Authentication is performed directly by the device operating system; Interna never accesses or stores biometric templates.
  • Files & Sharing: Used only when you choose to export PDF, DOCX, or CSV duty logs, or when creating/restoring a complete JSON backup file.
  • Internet Access: Used exclusively for optional AI report synthesis, rewarded ad delivery, backend credit validation, and viewing this privacy policy.

05

Data Governance

Retention & User Controls

Complete Data Control

You can edit or delete individual duty logs, photos, and settings anytime directly inside the app.

Full Device Erasure

Clearing application storage or uninstalling Interna immediately erases all locally stored records and photos from the device.

Data Portability & Backups

Export your full records to PDF, DOCX, CSV, or standalone JSON backup files for offline archiving or transferring to a new device.

Educational Responsibility

Interna is designed for internship record-keeping. Always respect workplace confidentiality and obtain consent before taking workplace photos or submitting logs.
