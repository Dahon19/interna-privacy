01 Who we are

OVERVIEW

Interna is an OJT (On-the-Job Training) management companion designed for students. It helps users track daily work logs, attach documentation proof photos, export structured reports, and generate narrative summary reports with AI assistance when connected online.

Most day-to-day tracking features operate entirely offline on your device. Internet access is exclusively required for online features such as AI generation attempts, rewarded ad credits, and associated security anti-abuse protections. Interna is independently developed and maintained by Dahon (View Developer Portfolio ↗).

02 What data Interna handles

DATA SCOPE

Interna is built around a privacy-conscious model. Here is a breakdown of data types stored locally or processed by the application:

Profile & Settings

User-entered profile details such as student name, course, school, company name, supervisor name, OJT date range, and total required target hours.

Work Logs & Notes

Work log dates, time ranges, task descriptions, quantities, captions, optional week-specific learning reflection notes, and saved narrative reports kept locally.

Documentation Photos

Proof images captured or chosen for log documentation. Photo files remain stored locally on device memory and are not uploaded during normal app operation.

Technical & Device Data

Install identifiers, device trust signals, app version details, Android identifiers, diagnostics, and request metadata necessary for protected AI and rewarded ads.

03 How data is used

PURPOSE
  • To operate core OJT logging, hour calculations, report generation, and document export features.
  • To enable offline-first tracking by storing all user logs directly on local device storage.
  • To construct AI narrative reports based on user-entered profile details, work logs, and week-specific learning reflection notes.
  • To manage rewarded-ad credit allocation and enforce weekly narrative generation limits.
  • To safeguard backend systems against automated abuse, reinstall fraud, and suspicious usage patterns.
  • To maintain application stability, performance diagnostics, and system reliability.

04 Ads, AI, and third-party services

INTEGRATIONS
⚡

Supabase Edge Services

Interna routes backend requests through Supabase Edge Functions for secure AI proxying, credit accounting, and rate-limiting anti-abuse verification.

🤖

Google Gemini, xAI Grok & NVIDIA

When narrative generation is triggered, prompt details (profile metadata, log entries, and learning notes) are submitted to backend AI providers (Google Gemini, xAI Grok, or NVIDIA hosted models) to generate formatted summary narratives.

📢

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

AdMob provides rewarded video ads for earning extra AI generation credits. AdMob SDK may collect device identifiers, app interaction data, diagnostics, and approximate coarse location derived from network context. Interna does not request precise GPS location.

05 Device trust & anti-abuse protections

SECURITY & TRUST

Interna utilizes technical integrity signals to prevent credit exploitation and preserve AI endpoint quotas. On supported Android builds, this incorporates Play Integrity verification, device trust metadata, install UUIDs, and request signature verification.

These anti-abuse measures detect repeated reinstall exploits, fraudulent credit claims, and suspicious API activity. This system operates autonomously without requiring user account registration or login password credentials.

06 How long data is retained

RETENTION
  • Local App Data: Logs, profile settings, proof photos, weekly notes, and exported PDFs remain on device storage until manually cleared or uninstalled.
  • Backend Logs: Anonymized request records and credit transaction counters are retained for quota enforcement, fraud prevention, and service diagnostics.
  • AI Service Providers: Prompts sent to AI backends are retained according to the respective operational and compliance policies of Google, xAI, or NVIDIA.
  • Backup & Restore: Local backup files contain user logs and learning notes. Backups do not restore AI credit state, which is tracked separately by backend trust systems.

07 Your controls and limitations

USER RIGHTS

Interna does not require account creation and does not maintain a centralized account deletion portal. You have complete direct control over your local data on your mobile device:

You can edit or delete log entries, remove photo attachments, wipe local application cache, or clear app data / uninstall the app at any time via Android system settings.

Protected operational records (e.g. backend quota state, anti-fraud trust counters, and rewarded credit logs) cannot be modified directly via local app deletion flows.

08 Security practices

ENCRYPTION

Interna enforces HTTPS / TLS encrypted network connections for all cloud interactions, including backend function calls, AI model streaming, and ad network requests. Standard technical protections are maintained to safeguard data in transit and guard against unauthorized API access.

09 Children and educational use

AUDIENCE

Interna is designed for senior high school, college, and university students undergoing internship or On-the-Job Training programs. It is not targeted at children under 13. Academic institutions and student users are encouraged to review this privacy policy prior to institutional deployment.
